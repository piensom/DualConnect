## Q&A Preparation

### Anticipated Questions

**Q: "How will you keep the data up-to-date?"**
A: "Great question. Our plan is to partner with IHK and companies for automatic data feeds. In the MVP, we have a manual update process, but we've designed the database to handle bulk imports."

**Q: "Is this available in other languages besides German and English?"**
A: "Currently, we support German and English. Our architecture is translation-ready, and we've identified Arabic, Turkish, and Russian as priority languages based on user research."

**Q: "How do you plan to monetize or sustain this?"**
A: "We see this as a public good. Potential sustainability models include partnerships with IHK/government funding, premium services for companies, or integration with existing platforms like Make it in Germany."

**Q: "Did you test with real international families?"**
A: "Yes, we conducted usability tests with 5 international families and 3 advisors. Their feedback directly shaped features like the parent section and simplified language."

**Q: "What was the biggest challenge?"**
A: "Balancing comprehensiveness with simplicity. We had to resist adding every possible feature and stay focused on the core user journey—search, understand, connect."

**Q: "What's next for the project?"**
A: "We're exploring partnerships with IHK Berlin and migration support organizations. We'd also like to build a mobile app and implement AI-powered program matching."

## Presentation Tips

### For Speakers
1. **Practice timing**: Rehearse to stay within 5-10 minutes
2. **Speak clearly**: Slower than you think, louder than feels natural
3. **Make eye contact**: Look at audience, not just slides
4. **Use pauses**: Let important points sink in
5. **Show enthusiasm**: Your passion is contagious

### For Team
1. **Divide slides**: Each person presents 2-3 slides
2. **Smooth transitions**: "Now [Name] will talk about..."
3. **Support each other**: Nod, smile, stay engaged
4. **Handle questions as a team**: "Let me pass that to [Name]"

### Technical Setup
1. **Arrive early**: Test equipment 15 minutes before
2. **Backup everything**: USB drive + cloud link + email
3. **Presenter mode**: Use if available (shows notes)
4. **Clicker**: Practice advancing slides smoothly
